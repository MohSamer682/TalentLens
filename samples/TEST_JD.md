# Job Description: Senior Backend Engineer (Java/Spring Boot)

**Location:** Remote / Hybrid  
**Stack:** Java 21, Spring Boot 3.x, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, AWS.

## About the Role
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our core platform team. You will be responsible for designing and implementing scalable microservices, optimizing database performance, and ensuring the high availability of our AI-driven services.

## Key Requirements
* **5+ years** of professional experience in Backend Development using **Java**.
* Deep expertise in **Spring Boot** (Spring Security, Spring Data JPA, Spring Cloud).
* Strong understanding of **PostgreSQL** and database optimization (indexing, partitioning).
* Experience with **Docker** and container orchestration (Kubernetes is a plus).
* Familiarity with **Redis** or other caching mechanisms.
* Proven track record of building and deploying RESTful APIs.

## Bonus Points
* Experience with LLM integration (OpenAI, LangChain).
* Knowledge of AWS (S3, RDS, Lambda).
